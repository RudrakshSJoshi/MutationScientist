The problem

EGFR mutations are why targeted lung-cancer drugs fail.

A clinician's drug works — until the tumour mutates the target. The real question a researcher needs answered, fast and honestly:

"Will drug Y still bind mutant X — and can I trust the answer?"

Prediction-only tools

give a number with no evidence and no honesty about where it breaks.
